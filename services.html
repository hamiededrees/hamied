I am different. Over the past seven years, I have built expertise across every major area of book marketing so that you do not have to hire five different freelancers and hope they all talk to each other. When you work with me, you get a single, unified book marketing strategy that covers every channel, every platform, and every touchpoint your readers encounter.

Below you will find detailed descriptions of all twelve services I offer. You can pick and choose the ones you need, or you can let me recommend a complete package based on your specific goals. Either way, every service is tailored to your book and your readers.

Core Services

Get Your Book Seen and Sold

These are the foundational book marketing services that drive the most immediate visibility and sales for your book.

1. Book Promotion & Advertising

This is the engine of your book marketing campaign. I design and manage targeted promotional campaigns across Amazon, Facebook, Instagram, BookBub, Goodreads, and Google to put your book directly in front of readers who are actively looking for books in your genre.

I handle everything from audience research and keyword targeting to ad copywriting, bid management, and daily performance optimization. You will not waste a single dollar on clicks from people who will never buy your book. Every campaign is laser-focused on conversions, not vanity metrics.

Platforms covered: Amazon Ads, Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads, BookBub Ads, Google Ads, Apple Search Ads

2. Social Media Book Marketing

Social media is where readers discover their next favorite book, and I know exactly how to position your book in the middle of those conversations. From TikTok BookTok trends to Instagram Reels, from Twitter threads to Pinterest boards, I create scroll-stopping content that makes readers stop, click, and buy.

I do not just post generic "buy my book" graphics and hope for the best. I study your genre's reader communities, I understand the content formats that perform best on each platform, and I create a social media book marketing calendar that builds genuine excitement around your book week after week.

Platforms covered: TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, Pinterest, YouTube, Threads

3. Book Launch Marketing

Launch week is the single most important window in your book's entire life. Amazon's algorithm rewards books that sell a high volume of copies in a short period, and a well-executed launch can catapult your book onto the bestseller charts and keep it there for weeks or even months.

My book launch marketing service includes a full pre-launch buildup starting four to six weeks before release day, a coordinated launch week blitz across all platforms, and a post-launch sustainment plan to keep your sales momentum going strong long after the initial excitement fades. I have launched over 250 books, and I know exactly what it takes to make launch day count.

Timeline: 4 to 8 weeks depending on your goals and budget

4. Amazon Ads & Paid Advertising

Amazon is the largest bookstore in the world, and Amazon Ads are the most powerful tool in any book marketer's arsenal when used correctly. The problem is that most authors set up their campaigns wrong, target the wrong keywords, bid too high or too low, and then give up after burning through their budget with nothing to show for it.

I manage Amazon Ads campaigns with surgical precision. I research hundreds of relevant keywords, I create multiple ad variations to test what resonates with your readers, I monitor your campaigns daily, and I continuously optimize your bids and targeting to maximize your return on every dollar spent. My average client sees a 3x to 7x return on their ad spend within the first eight weeks.

Ad types: Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Lockscreen Ads, DSP

Brand & Visuals

Build an Author Brand That Readers Remember

People buy from people they know and trust. These book marketing services focus on making you and your books instantly recognizable to your target audience.

5. Author Branding

Your author brand is the foundation of everything else in your book marketing. It is the reason a reader picks up your new release without even reading the description. It is the reason they follow you on social media, sign up for your email list, and recommend you to their friends.

I help you define your unique author voice, your visual identity, your messaging pillars, and your positioning within your genre. Whether you are a debut author building your brand from scratch or an established author looking for a refresh, I will create a cohesive brand strategy that makes you stand out in a crowded marketplace and keeps readers coming back for more.

Includes: Brand voice guide, visual identity, social media templates, author bio optimization, website messaging

6. Promotional Graphics & Visuals

In the world of book marketing, visuals are everything. A stunning 3D book mockup, an eye-catching social media graphic, a professional promotional banner, or a scroll-stopping ad creative can be the difference between a reader scrolling past your book and stopping to click "Learn More."

I create high-quality promotional visuals for every platform and every purpose: social media posts and stories, Facebook and Instagram ad creatives, Amazon A+ Content images, email headers, website banners, book fair displays, and more. Every graphic is designed to match your author brand and optimized for the specific dimensions and best practices of each platform.

Deliverables: Social media graphics, ad creatives, 3D mockups, banners, A+ Content, promo cards

7. Email Book Marketing

Email is still the most profitable channel in book marketing, period. Social media algorithms change every week, ad costs keep climbing, but your email list is an asset you own forever. The problem is that most authors either do not have an email list at all, or they have one that is collecting dust because they do not know what to send or how often to send it.

I build and manage email marketing campaigns that actually get opened, clicked, and converted. From lead magnet creation and landing page setup to welcome sequences, launch sequences, and ongoing reader engagement campaigns, I handle every aspect of your email book marketing so you can focus on writing your next book.

Includes: List building, lead magnets, welcome sequences, launch emails, monthly newsletters, re-engagement campaigns

8. Reader & Influencer Outreach

One of the fastest ways to get your book in front of thousands of new readers is through strategic outreach to book influencers, book bloggers, BookTok creators, Bookstagram accounts, podcast hosts, and YouTube book reviewers who already have the attention of your target audience.

I maintain an extensive network of book influencers across every major genre and platform. I handle the entire outreach process from identifying the right influencers for your book, crafting personalized pitch emails, coordinating review copies, negotiating partnerships, and tracking the results of every collaboration. You get the exposure without the awkward cold-emailing.

Channels: BookTok, Bookstagram, book blogs, YouTube, podcasts, Goodreads groups, Facebook reader groups

Optimization

Fine-Tune Every Detail for Maximum Sales

Sometimes the smallest changes in your book marketing setup can produce the biggest jumps in sales. These services focus on optimizing what you already have.

9. Book Description & Marketing Copy

Your book description is your single most important piece of marketing copy. It is the one thing every potential buyer reads before deciding whether to click "Buy Now" or scroll to the next book. A weak description kills sales no matter how good your cover is or how much you spend on ads.

I write compelling, conversion-optimized book descriptions that hook readers from the very first line and compel them to take action. I also write ad copy, email copy, social media captions, press releases, and any other marketing copy you need. Every word is crafted with one goal in mind: making readers buy your book.

Includes: Amazon book description, ad copy, email copy, social media copy, press releases, back cover copy

10. Review Generation Strategy

Reviews are the lifeblood of book sales on Amazon and every other retailer. A book with 200 five-star reviews will outsell a book with 5 reviews almost every single time, even if the second book is objectively better. Readers trust other readers, and they use reviews as social proof before committing their money and their time.

I build ethical, Amazon-compliant review generation strategies that help you accumulate genuine reader reviews at a steady pace. This includes ARC team coordination, reviewer outreach, follow-up email sequences, and strategic timing around your launch and promotional periods. No fake reviews, no review swaps, no policy violations. Just real readers sharing honest opinions about your book.

Methods: ARC teams, BookSirens, NetGalley, reviewer outreach, reader magnet funnels, follow-up sequences

11. Amazon Listing Optimization

Your Amazon book listing is your storefront. If it is not optimized for both the Amazon algorithm and human readers, you are leaving money on the table every single day. I have seen authors double their organic sales just by fixing their categories, keywords, and A+ Content without spending a single extra dollar on advertising.

I conduct a full audit of your Amazon listing and optimize every element: title and subtitle structure, backend keywords, category selection, book description HTML formatting, A+ Content design, author page optimization, and pricing strategy. I make sure that when a reader searches for books in your genre, your book shows up at the top of the results.

Includes: Keyword research, category optimization, A+ Content, author page, backend metadata, pricing analysis

12. Full Book Marketing Strategy

This is the ultimate book marketing service for authors who want a complete, done-for-you marketing plan that covers every channel, every platform, and every phase of their book's lifecycle. Think of it as having a personal Chief Marketing Officer for your book career.

I start with a deep-dive analysis of your book, your genre, your competitors, and your target readers. Then I build a comprehensive book marketing roadmap that covers the next three to twelve months, including specific tactics, timelines, budgets, and expected outcomes for every single activity. I then execute the entire plan for you, handling every detail so you can focus on what you do best: writing great books.

Duration: 3 to 12 months, fully customized to your goals and budget
