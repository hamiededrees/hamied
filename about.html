Professional book marketer, author advocate, and your dedicated partner in turning great books into bestselling realities.

My Story

How I Became a Book Marketer

Seven years ago, a close friend published her first novel. She had poured two years of her life into writing it, hired a professional editor, invested in a beautiful cover, and hit the publish button with tears of joy in her eyes. Then reality set in. After three months, she had sold fewer than forty copies. She was devastated, and honestly, so was I.

That moment lit a fire inside me. I started studying everything I could find about book marketing: Amazon algorithms, Facebook ad targeting, BookBub featured deals, email list building, social media reader communities, and the psychology behind why readers click "Buy Now" on one book but scroll past another.

Fast forward to today, and I have personally managed book marketing campaigns for over 250 authors across more than 30 countries. I have helped first-time self-publishers land their first thousand sales, and I have helped mid-career authors break out of plateaus they thought were permanent. Book marketing is not just what I do for a living. It is my calling.

My Mission

Why I Am Obsessed With Book Marketing

Every day, thousands of talented authors publish incredible books that nobody ever finds. The stories are powerful, the writing is polished, the covers are stunning, but the book marketing is either nonexistent or completely misaligned with the target audience. That gap between a great book and its readers is the problem I wake up every morning determined to solve.

I believe that every author, regardless of their budget, their genre, their country, or their language, deserves a fair shot at reaching the readers who will love their work. The publishing industry has changed dramatically over the last decade, and the old gatekeepers no longer control who gets discovered. But the new landscape is noisy, competitive, and confusing, and that is exactly where a skilled book marketer makes all the difference.

My mission is simple: I want to be the bridge between your book and the readers who are already searching for it but have not found it yet. That is what drives every campaign I build, every ad I optimize, and every strategy I design.

My Philosophy

The Book Marketing Approach That Actually Works

After seven years and hundreds of campaigns, I have distilled my book marketing philosophy down to a few core principles that guide everything I do for my authors.

Data First, Gut Second

Every decision I make is backed by real data. I analyze your Amazon sales rank, your ad click-through rates, your social media engagement metrics, your email open rates, and your reader demographics before I recommend a single tactic. Book marketing without data is just guessing, and guessing is expensive.

Long-Term Growth Over Quick Tricks

I am not interested in giving you a temporary spike that disappears in two weeks. My book marketing strategies are designed to build sustainable, compounding growth that keeps delivering results long after our campaign officially ends. I want your tenth book to sell better than your first, and I build the foundation to make that happen.

Complete Transparency

You will never wonder where your money is going or what I am actually doing. I provide detailed weekly reports, I am available for regular check-in calls, and I explain every strategy in plain language. No jargon, no smoke and mirrors, just honest book marketing that delivers.
