# Hamied A. Edrees | Professional Book Marketer

Professional book marketing website for Hamied A. Edrees, a book marketer with 7 years of experience and 250+ books promoted worldwide.

## Pages

- **Home** (index.html) - Main landing page with hero, services preview, testimonials preview, and statistics
- **About** (about.html) - Full biography, story, promise, and why choose me section
- **Services** (services.html) - Complete list of 12 book marketing services with genres and process
- **Packages** (packages.html) - Three pricing tiers: Starter ($297), Growth ($697), Premium ($1,497)
- **Testimonials** (testimonials.html) - 9 detailed author testimonials from worldwide clients
- **FAQs** (faq.html) - 12 frequently asked questions with detailed answers
- **Contact** (contact.html) - Contact form, details, social links, and next steps

## Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3 (Custom properties, Grid, Flexbox, Animations)
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Google Fonts (Playfair Display, Inter)
- Font Awesome 6.5 Icons

## Setup

1. Clone this repository
2. Add your images to the `/images/` folder:
   - `logo.png` - Your business logo
   - `HIA.png` - Your professional headshot
   - `banner.png` - Hero section background image
3. Update contact information (email, phone, social links) across all pages
4. Deploy to GitHub Pages

## Deployment to GitHub Pages

1. Go to repository Settings
2. Navigate to Pages section
3. Under Source, select "Deploy from a branch"
4. Select "main" branch and "/ (root)" folder
5. Click Save
6. Your site will be live at `https://yourusername.github.io/repository-name/`
