Featured Book Marketing Case Study

Here is a closer look at one of my most successful book marketing campaigns to give you a real sense of what working with me looks like from start to finish.

From 5 Sales a Week to Amazon Bestseller in 8 Weeks

The Author: Sarah Reynolds, a mid-career romance author from Texas who had published three novels but was stuck at around five to ten sales per week across all her titles. She had tried running Amazon Ads on her own but was losing money every month.

The Problem: Her Amazon listings were not optimized, her ad targeting was too broad, her book descriptions were not converting browsers into buyers, and she had zero social media presence as an author. Her books were essentially invisible to the millions of romance readers actively searching for their next read.

The Book Marketing Strategy: I completely rewrote her Amazon listings with conversion-optimized descriptions and backend keywords. I rebuilt her Amazon Ads campaigns from scratch with hyper-targeted keyword lists and competitor targeting. I launched Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns aimed at specific romance reader communities. I coordinated an ARC review campaign that generated 45 new reviews in the first month. And I set up a simple email funnel that started building her reader list from day one.

The Result: Within eight weeks, Sarah went from five sales a week to over two hundred. Her latest novel hit the top 500 in historical romance on Amazon, her email list grew from zero to over 1,200 subscribers, and her backlist sales increased by 300 percent thanks to the halo effect. She has since enrolled in two more book marketing campaigns with me and is now a full-time author.
