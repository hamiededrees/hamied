How Long Does a Book Marketing Campaign Take?

Every book is different, and every author has different goals. Here is a breakdown of the typical campaign durations I recommend based on your specific situation.

4 Weeks: The Quick Boost

A four-week book marketing campaign is ideal if you have a specific short-term goal, like boosting sales during a promotional pricing window, generating initial reviews for a new release, or testing which ad platforms perform best for your genre before committing to a longer campaign.

Best for: New releases, limited-time promotions, ad testing, review generation sprints, and authors on a tight budget who want to see what professional book marketing can do for them.

8 Weeks: The Growth Sprint

Eight weeks is the sweet spot for most book marketing campaigns. It gives us enough time to launch multiple ad platforms, test and optimize your campaigns based on real data, build momentum on social media, and start seeing compounding results that continue growing even after the campaign ends.

Best for: Book launches, mid-career authors looking to break through a sales plateau, authors expanding into new markets or genres, and anyone who wants a comprehensive multi-platform push.

12 Weeks: The Full Transformation

A twelve-week book marketing campaign is a complete career investment. We have time to build your author brand from the ground up, launch and optimize campaigns across every relevant platform, build your email list, secure influencer partnerships, generate a strong review base, and create a sustainable growth engine that keeps delivering results long after our work together is done.

Best for: Serious authors building a long-term career, full book launches with pre-order campaigns, authors with multiple books in their backlist, and anyone ready to dominate their genre.
